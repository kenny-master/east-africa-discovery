A REGION OF REMARKABLE CONTRASTS

One region.
Many worlds.

From the Indian Ocean to the great lakes, East Africa is a mosaic of landscapes, languages, flavours and traditions. Travel slowly, meet local makers, taste regional dishes and experience the stories that make every place distinct.

This guide uses the eight East African Community partner states: Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Rwanda, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania and Uganda. Other geographic definitions of Eastern Africa may include additional countries.

CULTURE IS THE JOURNEY

Meet the region
through its people.

Beyond the landmarks are living traditions: a drumbeat carried across generations, a recipe shared around a table, a pattern woven by hand, a song that brings a city to life.

01 · MUSIC & MOVEMENT

Taarab, bongo flava, Congolese rumba, traditional drumming, gospel and contemporary sounds.

02 · CRAFT & CREATIVITY

Basketry, beadwork, textiles, barkcloth, wood carving, painting and contemporary design.

03 · FOOD & GATHERING

Shared plates, spice markets, coffee, grilled fish, regional stews and street-food culture.

Cultures are diverse within every country. We encourage respectful, community-led experiences rather than treating any tradition as a single story.
